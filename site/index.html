Everything for notes – in your Nextcloud

Next Notes brings a full notes app into Nextcloud: same login, same users, same teams, same storage.

  • Notes and editor

    Rich text and Markdown, task lists, Excalidraw drawings, math formulas, diagrams and code. Tables that calculate and a calculator block with function graphs.

  • Workspaces and teams

    Private workspaces, workspaces shared with single people (read only or edit) and team workspaces of a Nextcloud team. Folders, tags, attachments, revisions and trash.

  • Assignments

    Hand out a note with a deadline. Every student works on an own copy, submits it and gets it back with feedback and points.

  • OneNote import

    Users bring their OneNote notebooks with images and files into their own workspaces, set up once by the school's administrator.

  • Nextcloud integration

    Nextcloud login, teams, notifications for reminders and @-mentions, unified search, user migration and occ commands. Light and dark mode follow your theme.

  • Privacy

    Data stays in your Nextcloud database and data directory. No tracking, no third parties, no AI features. Every request is checked against user and team permissions.

Made for schools

Teachers are team moderators, students are team members. That is all the setup a class needs.

  1. Create a team

    Create a Nextcloud team for the class in the Contacts app and add the students.

  2. Open a team workspace

    Everyone in the team can read and write. Access ends when someone leaves the team.

  3. Publish material

    Mark a folder as material: teachers edit it, students read it and can copy notes for themselves.

  4. Hand out and return

    Students work on their own copy and submit it before the deadline. Teachers give feedback and points and return it.

Submissions stay with the teachers when a student leaves the team or the account is deleted. They are removed only when a teacher deletes the assignment or the team is deleted.

Requirements

  • Nextcloud 35
  • PHP 8.3 – 8.5
  • MariaDB/MySQL, PostgreSQL or SQLite
  • Background jobs via system cron
  • Optional: Teams app for team workspaces and assignments, Contacts app to manage teams

Installation

  1. App Store (once published): Apps → Office & text → Next Notes.
  2. Release package: extract nextnotes-<version>.tar.gz into custom_apps/ and run occ app:enable nextnotes.
  3. From source: npm ci --prefix nextnotes/build and make -C nextnotes dist.

Read the administration guide and the update notes (both in German).

Based on Poznote

Next Notes is based on Poznote by Timothé Poznanski. The editor, the notes interface and many ideas come from Poznote; Next Notes replaces its backend with a Nextcloud app and adds teams, material and assignments. Thank you, Timothé!
